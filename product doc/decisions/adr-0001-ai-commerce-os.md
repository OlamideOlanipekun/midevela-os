# ADR-0001: AI Commerce Operating System

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-06-29

---

# Context

Most commerce platforms separate products, customer support, CRM, marketing, analytics, and AI into independent systems.

Businesses are forced to switch between multiple tools, creating fragmented customer experiences and duplicated data.

---

# Decision

Midevela will be designed as an **AI Commerce Operating System**, where AI is the central orchestration layer connecting every commerce function.

Instead of adding AI as a feature, AI becomes the platform itself.

---

# Consequences

Positive

- Unified customer experience
- Single source of truth
- Consistent AI decisions
- Lower operational complexity
- Easier automation

Trade-offs

- Higher initial architecture complexity
- Greater infrastructure requirements
- Larger platform scope

---

# Alternatives Considered

- Traditional CRM
- AI chatbot platform
- Marketing automation tool
- Customer support software

Rejected because none provide a unified commerce intelligence platform.

---

# Outcome

This decision establishes the foundation for every future architectural choice.
