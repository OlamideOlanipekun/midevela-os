# The Competitive Landscape

The market is crowded with "chatbots" and "customer support" tools. Midevela's differentiation is positioning as a **proactive AI Sales Assistant**, not a reactive support bot.

## Category 1: Legacy Customer Support Bots
*Examples: Intercom, Zendesk, Gorgias*

- **Their focus:** Ticket deflection, answering "Where is my order?", human-agent routing.
- **Their weakness:** They wait for the customer to complain. They are reactive. They do not proactively guide product discovery or increase buying confidence.
- **Midevela's Advantage:** We focus on pre-purchase conversion, intent prediction, and active selling. We make money for the business; they save money for the business.

## Category 2: Traditional E-commerce Search & Merchandising
*Examples: Algolia, Klevu, Nosto*

- **Their focus:** Improving the search bar, category filtering, and product carousels ("Customers who bought this also bought...").
- **Their weakness:** They still require the customer to know what they are looking for. They don't engage in natural dialogue or handle objections.
- **Midevela's Advantage:** Conversational discovery. The customer describes their *problem*, and Midevela recommends the *solution* with transparent reasoning.

## Category 3: Emerging AI Commerce Chatbots
*Examples: Rep.ai, Certainly, Tidio AI, Shopify Sidekick*

- **Their focus:** Adding LLMs to store chat widgets to recommend products based on catalog data.
- **Their weakness:** Often single-channel (website only). Lacks deep "Buying Confidence" tracking. Often hallucinates if not strictly constrained.
- **Midevela's Advantage:** 
  1. **Omnichannel Memory:** The conversation persists across Instagram, WhatsApp, and the website.
  2. **Confidence Scoring:** We measure and optimize for buying confidence, explicitly tackling hesitation rather than just spitting out product links.
  3. **AI Sales Coach:** We coach the business on missing knowledge and revenue leaks.

# Strategic Positioning

When selling Midevela, we **never** compare ourselves to Intercom or Zendesk. 
We position against **lost revenue** and **high customer acquisition costs (CAC)**.

**The Pitch:** "You are spending $10,000 a month on ads to drive traffic, but 97% of visitors leave because they are confused or lack confidence. Midevela is the AI salesperson that talks to that 97%, builds their confidence, and turns them into buyers."
