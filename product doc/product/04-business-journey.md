# Purpose

This document defines the lifecycle of a business using Midevela—from signup to becoming an AI-powered commerce organization.

The business journey informs onboarding, dashboard workflows, customer success, and long-term product adoption.

---

# Business Lifecycle

```text
Discover

↓

Sign Up

↓

Connect Website

↓

Import Knowledge

↓

Configure AI

↓

Launch

↓

Monitor

↓

Optimize

↓

Scale
```

---

# Stage 1 — Discover

The business learns about Midevela.

Typical motivations:

* Low conversion
* High support costs
* Poor customer engagement
* Difficulty scaling sales

---

# Stage 2 — Sign Up

The business creates an account.

Information collected:

* Business name
* Industry
* Website
* Team size
* Country

The onboarding experience should be completed in minutes.

---

# Stage 3 — Connect Website

Businesses connect their website through supported integrations or install the Midevela widget.

Goals:

* Verify ownership
* Enable data synchronization
* Prepare AI deployment

---

# Stage 4 — Import Knowledge

Knowledge sources may include:

* Product catalog
* Website pages
* FAQs
* Policies
* Existing documentation
* Manual uploads

The Knowledge Engine creates the Business Brain.

---

# Stage 5 — Configure AI

Businesses configure:

* Brand tone
* Greeting style
* Business hours
* Escalation rules
* Supported languages
* Sales objectives

The AI becomes an extension of the brand.

---

# Stage 6 — Launch

The AI Shopping Assistant becomes available across enabled channels.

Customers begin interacting immediately.

Analytics collection starts from the first conversation.

---

# Stage 7 — Monitor

Business owners access dashboards showing:

* Active visitors
* AI conversations
* Recommendations
* Conversion metrics
* Customer questions
* AI performance

Insights are presented in business language rather than technical metrics.

---

# Stage 8 — Optimize

The platform recommends improvements such as:

* Missing product information
* Frequently unanswered questions
* New knowledge to add
* Better recommendation strategies
* Conversation improvements

Businesses continuously improve through AI insights.

---

# Stage 9 — Scale

As the business grows, Midevela supports:

* Multiple team members
* Additional channels
* Multiple brands
* New markets
* Enterprise integrations

The AI scales without requiring proportional growth in support staff.

---

# Business Success Metrics

* AI Adoption Rate
* Website Conversion Rate
* Revenue Influenced by AI
* Support Cost Reduction
* Knowledge Coverage
* Customer Satisfaction
* Repeat Customer Rate

---

# Product Implications

Every onboarding decision should reduce the time between signup and first successful AI conversation.

Time-to-Value is a core product metric.
