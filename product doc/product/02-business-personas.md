# Purpose

This document defines the primary business customers that Midevela is designed to serve.

Unlike **User Personas**, which describe shoppers, Business Personas describe the organizations that purchase and operate Midevela.

Understanding these personas helps prioritize features, pricing, onboarding, integrations, and future roadmap decisions.

---

# Primary Customer

Midevela serves businesses that want to improve customer experience and increase online revenue by providing AI-powered shopping guidance.

The platform is industry-agnostic but initially targets ecommerce-first businesses.

---

# Business Persona 1 — Growing Ecommerce Brand

## Profile

A fast-growing online business with an existing website and an expanding product catalog.

Examples include:

* Fashion brands
* Beauty brands
* Electronics stores
* Furniture stores
* Health and wellness brands

---

## Characteristics

* 50–10,000 products
* Existing ecommerce website
* Growing marketing spend
* Limited customer support team
* Looking to increase conversion rather than traffic

---

## Pain Points

* High bounce rate
* Cart abandonment
* Repetitive customer questions
* Customers cannot find products
* Low conversion despite strong traffic

---

## Goals

* Increase conversion rate
* Reduce support workload
* Improve customer experience
* Increase average order value

---

## Success Metrics

* Revenue growth
* Conversion improvement
* AI-assisted sales
* Reduced support tickets

---

# Business Persona 2 — Service Business

## Profile

Businesses selling services rather than physical products.

Examples include:

* Agencies
* SaaS companies
* Consulting firms
* Training providers
* Healthcare providers

---

## Challenges

Visitors often don't understand:

* Available services
* Pricing
* Which plan fits them
* Booking process

---

## AI Responsibilities

The AI should:

* Qualify leads
* Explain services
* Recommend plans
* Schedule consultations
* Capture leads

---

# Business Persona 3 — Multi-Location Business

## Profile

Organizations operating across multiple branches or regions.

Examples

* Retail chains
* Hotel groups
* Restaurant franchises
* Healthcare networks

---

## Requirements

* Central management
* Location-specific knowledge
* Shared analytics
* Team permissions
* Consistent customer experience

---

# Business Persona 4 — Enterprise

## Profile

Large organizations with mature digital operations.

Characteristics include:

* Large catalogs
* Multiple departments
* Dedicated support teams
* Existing CRM systems
* Existing ERP systems

---

## Enterprise Needs

* API access
* SSO
* Role-based permissions
* Audit logs
* Advanced analytics
* Compliance
* Custom integrations

---

# Business Persona 5 — Digital Creator

## Profile

Individuals or small teams selling:

* Digital products
* Courses
* Memberships
* Templates
* E-books
* Coaching

---

## Challenges

Limited time.

Limited support.

Need automated selling.

---

## AI Responsibilities

* Recommend products
* Answer common questions
* Upsell relevant products
* Guide purchases
* Build trust

---

# Common Business Problems

Across every persona, businesses face similar challenges:

* Visitors leave without buying.
* Customers struggle to find products.
* Sales teams answer repetitive questions.
* Customer acquisition costs are increasing.
* Valuable customer insights are lost.
* Existing websites are passive.

---

# Why Businesses Choose Midevela

Businesses invest in Midevela because they want to transform their websites from digital catalogs into intelligent sales experiences.

Instead of simply displaying information, the website actively participates in converting visitors into customers.

---

# Product Implications

These personas influence:

* Integrations
* Analytics

Every roadmap decision should consider its impact on at least one primary business persona.
