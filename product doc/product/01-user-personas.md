# Purpose

This document defines the primary users who interact with Midevela.

Understanding these personas ensures every feature solves real customer problems instead of being driven by assumptions.

Every feature proposal should identify which persona it primarily serves.

---

# Persona Categories

Midevela serves two broad groups:

1. Businesses (customers of Midevela)
2. Their Customers (shoppers interacting with the AI)

This document focuses on the second group—the end users of the shopping experience.

---

# Persona 1 — The Explorer

## Profile

The Explorer is browsing without a specific product in mind.

They know the outcome they want but are unsure which product can achieve it.

Examples

* "I want healthier skin."
* "I need a laptop for programming."
* "I need a birthday gift."
* "I'm looking for running shoes."

---

## Pain Points

* Too many products
* Doesn't understand technical specifications
* Doesn't know where to begin
* Feels overwhelmed

---

## Goals

* Discover suitable products
* Learn quickly
* Save time
* Feel confident

---

## AI Responsibilities

The AI should:

* Ask discovery questions
* Narrow options
* Explain recommendations
* Build confidence

---

# Persona 2 — The Researcher

## Profile

The Researcher already knows several possible products.

They compare before buying.

---

## Typical Questions

* What's the difference?
* Which is better?
* Is it worth paying more?
* Which lasts longer?
* Which fits my needs?

---

## Pain Points

* Too much information
* Marketing language
* Hard comparisons
* Lack of expert guidance

---

## Goals

* Make the best decision
* Reduce risk
* Understand trade-offs

---

## AI Responsibilities

The AI should:

* Compare products
* Explain differences
* Recommend based on needs
* Provide evidence

---

# Persona 3 — The Ready Buyer

## Profile

The Ready Buyer already knows what they want.

They simply need to purchase quickly.

---

## Pain Points

* Slow checkout
* Difficulty finding product
* Stock uncertainty
* Shipping questions

---

## Goals

* Purchase immediately
* Complete checkout
* Receive reassurance

---

## AI Responsibilities

The AI should:

* Detect purchase intent
* Reduce friction
* Answer last-minute questions
* Encourage completion

---

# Persona 4 — The Returning Customer

## Profile

Already purchased from the business.

Returns for another purchase.

---

## Expectations

* Remember previous purchases
* Personalized recommendations
* Faster conversations
* Relevant promotions

---

## AI Responsibilities

* Use customer memory
* Continue previous conversations
* Recommend complementary products
* Build loyalty

---

# Persona 5 — The Hesitant Customer

## Profile

Interested but uncertain.

Likely objections include:

* Price
* Trust
* Features
* Quality
* Compatibility

---

## Goals

Reduce perceived risk.

---

## AI Responsibilities

The AI should identify objections early and address them with truthful, contextual information while avoiding pressure.

---

# Shared User Expectations

Regardless of persona, customers expect:

* Fast responses
* Honest information
* Personalized guidance
* Natural conversations
* Helpful recommendations
* Consistent experiences

---

# Success Metrics

This document influences:

* Conversation completion
* Recommendation acceptance
* Customer satisfaction
* Conversion rate
* Time to purchase

---

# Design Implications

Every shopping experience should support all five personas without requiring customers to choose a mode manually.

The AI should infer the persona from customer behavior and adapt accordingly.
