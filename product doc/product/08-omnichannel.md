# Purpose

This document defines how Midevela delivers a single, continuous customer experience across every supported communication channel.

Customers should never feel like they are talking to different systems.

The conversation should persist regardless of where it began.

---

# Core Principle

**One Customer**
↓
**One Memory**
↓
**One Conversation**
↓
**Many Channels**

The channel changes.

The conversation does not.

---

# Why Omnichannel Matters

Today's customer journey is fragmented.

A typical buying journey might look like:

```text
Google Search
↓
Website
↓
Instagram
↓
WhatsApp
↓
Email
↓
Website Checkout
```

Traditional systems treat each interaction as unrelated.

Midevela treats them as one continuous experience.

---

# Unified Customer Memory

Every customer has a single profile.

It contains:

- Conversation history
- Product interests
- Preferences
- Purchase history
- Saved recommendations
- Objections
- AI confidence score
- Buying stage

All channels reference the same profile.

---

# Conversation Continuity

Example

Website:

> I'm looking for a gaming laptop.

Customer leaves.

Later...

WhatsApp:

> Hi.

AI:

> Welcome back. Last time we narrowed your search to three gaming laptops under ₦2,000,000. Would you like to continue?

No repeated questions.

No lost context.

---

# Identity Resolution

Customers may be identified through:

- Login
- Email
- Phone number
- WhatsApp account
- Session linking
- Customer confirmation

When confidence is low, the AI asks before merging identities.

---

# Cross-Channel Actions

The AI can:

- Send checkout links
- Continue recommendations
- Share saved comparisons
- Resume unfinished conversations
- Notify customers about relevant updates
- Escalate to human agents

All while preserving context.

---

# Business Benefits

Businesses gain:

- Higher conversion rates
- Better customer understanding
- Reduced support repetition
- Unified analytics
- Stronger customer relationships

---

# Technical Principles

The omnichannel layer must provide:

- Shared Customer Memory
- Shared Knowledge Engine
- Shared Recommendation Engine
- Shared Conversation State
- Shared Analytics

Channels become interfaces—not isolated systems.

---

# Privacy & Consent

Customer information must be handled responsibly.

The platform should:

- Respect consent preferences
- Support data deletion
- Allow profile management
- Maintain audit trails
- Comply with applicable privacy regulations

---

# Success Metrics

- Cross-Channel Continuation Rate
- Returning Customer Recognition
- Conversation Completion Rate
- Customer Satisfaction
- Repeat Purchase Rate
- Identity Resolution Accuracy

---

# Future Vision

A customer should be able to start on one device, continue on another, switch channels multiple times, and never lose momentum.

The AI remembers so the customer doesn't have to.

---

# Summary

Omnichannel is not about connecting channels.

It is about creating one continuous relationship between the customer and the business.
