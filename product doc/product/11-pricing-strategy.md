# Purpose

This document outlines the monetization strategy for Midevela. Pricing must align with our core value proposition: **increasing conversion and generating revenue.** 

If we help businesses sell more, our pricing should scale with their success.

---

# Pricing Philosophy

1. **Value-Based:** Businesses should pay in proportion to the value (conversations/revenue) Midevela generates for them.
2. **Low Barrier to Entry:** The product must be easy to try and validate on a live store.
3. **Transparent:** No hidden fees. Customers should easily predict their monthly costs.

---

# The Pricing Model

We utilize a **Hybrid SaaS Model**: a base platform fee + usage-based pricing on successful AI interactions.

## 1. Free Tier (Starter / Validation)
*Designed for small stores and initial validation.*
- **Price:** $0 / ₦0
- **Includes:**
  - Up to 100 AI conversations per month
  - 1 Sales Channel (Website Widget only)
  - Up to 500 products synced
  - Standard Support
- **Overage:** Pauses AI chat and falls back to standard lead capture, or prompts upgrade.

## 2. Growth Tier (Core SaaS)
*Designed for growing e-commerce brands.*
- **Price:** $49 / ₦50,000 per month
- **Includes:**
  - Up to 1,000 AI conversations per month
  - Omnichannel (Widget + WhatsApp + Instagram)
  - Up to 5,000 products synced
  - Advanced Analytics & AI Coaching
- **Overage:** $0.05 / ₦50 per additional conversation.

## 3. Scale Tier
*Designed for high-volume stores.*
- **Price:** $199 / ₦200,000 per month
- **Includes:**
  - Up to 5,000 AI conversations per month
  - All channels
  - Unlimited products
  - Dedicated Success Manager & API access
- **Overage:** $0.03 / ₦30 per additional conversation.

## 4. Enterprise (Custom)
*Designed for multi-location, multi-brand, or massive catalogs.*
- Custom SLA, SSO, dedicated infrastructure, and custom RAG tuning.

# Why "Per Conversation" Pricing?

We charge per *meaningful conversation* rather than per *token* or per *API call*. 
- A "Conversation" is defined as a session where the user sends at least one message and receives an AI response.
- This shields the business from LLM token complexity.
- It directly aligns with value: a conversation is a qualified lead or a guided sales session.

---

# Metrics to Monitor Post-Launch

- **Conversion Rate to Paid:** Target > 15% from Free to Growth.
- **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):** We must monitor average LLM token cost per conversation to ensure the $0.05 overage charge maintains a >70% gross margin.
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Payback Period:** Target < 3 months.
