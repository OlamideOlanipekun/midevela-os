# Purpose

This document provides a high-level overview of the Midevela platform.

It defines what the product is, who it serves, the problems it solves, and how its major systems work together to deliver an intelligent commerce experience.

Every product, engineering, design, marketing, and customer success team member should read this document before working on any part of the platform.

---

# What is Midevela?

Midevela is an **AI Commerce Operating System**.

It transforms traditional websites into intelligent shopping experiences capable of understanding visitors, guiding buying decisions, answering questions, recommending products, and continuously learning from customer interactions.

Unlike traditional ecommerce tools that simply organize products, Midevela actively participates in every buying journey.

---

# Product Vision

Traditional ecommerce focuses on displaying products.

Midevela focuses on helping customers make confident purchasing decisions.

Instead of building better search experiences, we build better buying experiences.

---

# The Problem

Today's online stores expect customers to do all the work.

Customers must:

- Search
- Browse
- Compare
- Read specifications
- Understand technical terms
- Decide alone

This leads to:

- Buying confusion
- Analysis paralysis
- High bounce rates
- Cart abandonment
- Lost revenue
- Increased support workload

Businesses invest heavily in acquiring traffic but lose many visitors because websites fail to guide them.

---

# Our Solution

Midevela introduces an AI-powered sales layer that sits between the customer and the business.

The platform:

- Understands customer intent.
- Starts meaningful conversations.
- Learns customer preferences.
- Recommends the right products.
- Explains recommendations.
- Answers objections.
- Assists throughout checkout.
- Learns from every completed interaction.

The result is an experience that feels less like browsing a website and more like speaking with an experienced salesperson.

---

# Core Product Components

The platform consists of five major layers.

## 1. Business Intelligence Layer

Stores and organizes everything the AI needs to know about the business.

Examples:

- Products
- Categories
- Inventory
- Policies
- FAQs
- Brand voice
- Promotions

---

## 2. Customer Intelligence Layer

Builds a unified understanding of every customer.

Examples:

- Preferences
- Conversation history
- Purchase history
- Behavior
- Interests
- Buying stage

---

## 3. AI Decision Layer

The reasoning engine.

Responsible for:

- Intent detection
- Recommendation generation
- Question answering
- Objection handling
- Next Best Action selection

---

## 4. Experience Layer

Where customers interact with the AI.

Includes:

- Website widget
- WhatsApp
- Instagram
- Facebook
- Email
- Future channels

Customers receive one continuous experience regardless of entry point.

---

## 5. Business Platform

The internal dashboard used by businesses.

Provides:

- Analytics
- Customer insights
- Conversation history
- Knowledge management
- Product management
- Integrations
- AI performance
- Revenue insights
