# Purpose

This document defines how shopping should feel inside every Midevela-powered business.

Customers should never feel like they are browsing a catalogue.

Instead, they should feel like they have a knowledgeable salesperson walking with them throughout their buying journey.

---

# Core Philosophy

Shopping is not searching.

Shopping is decision making.

Every feature inside Midevela exists to help customers make better decisions with less effort.

---

# Traditional Shopping

Most ecommerce websites expect customers to:

- Search
- Filter
- Compare
- Read
- Guess
- Buy

The website remains passive.

---

# Midevela Shopping

Midevela changes the flow into:

Customer arrives
↓
AI understands intent
↓
Conversation begins
↓
Needs discovered
↓
Products shortlisted
↓
Questions answered
↓
Confidence increases
↓
Purchase completed

# The Five Shopping Principles

## 1. Every visitor receives assistance

Nobody shops alone.

Whether the store has:

- 5 visitors
- 500 visitors
- 50,000 visitors

Everyone receives guidance.

---

## 2. Recommendations explain themselves

Instead of saying

> Buy Product A

The AI says

> I recommend Product A because it fits your budget, your intended use, and the features you said were important.

Recommendations should always be transparent.

---

## 3. Questions become conversations

Instead of isolated answers:

Customer:

> Does this work for oily skin?

AI:

> Yes. Since you mentioned oily skin, this product helps reduce excess oil while remaining gentle enough for daily use. Would you like me to compare it with another option?

---

## 4. The AI adapts

The experience changes depending on:

- Visitor behaviour
- Customer memory
- Purchase history
- Session activity
- Referral source
- Device
- Intent

No two shopping experiences are identical.

---

## 5. The AI never pressures

The goal is confidence.

Not manipulation.

If the customer is uncertain,
the AI should educate rather than persuade.

---

# Shopping Modes

## Discovery Mode

Customer has no idea what to buy.

The AI asks questions.

---

## Comparison Mode

Customer compares multiple products.

The AI highlights meaningful differences.

---

## Expert Mode

Customer already knows what they want.

The AI minimizes friction.

---

## Returning Customer Mode

Customer has history.

The AI remembers preferences and recommends accordingly.

---

# AI Responsibilities

Throughout shopping, the AI should:

- Answer questions
- Explain products
- Detect confusion
- Recommend products
- Handle objections
- Upsell responsibly
- Cross-sell when relevant
- Guide checkout
- Continue after purchase

---

# Measuring Shopping Success

Key metrics include:

- Recommendation Acceptance Rate
- Time to Recommendation
- Time to Purchase
- AI Conversation Completion
- Average Order Value
- Conversion Rate
- Customer Satisfaction

---

# Design Principles

Shopping interfaces should be:

- Fast
- Conversational
- Mobile-first
- Accessible
- Human-centered
- Explainable

# Summary

Midevela transforms shopping from a self-service experience into a guided buying experience.

Every interaction should reduce uncertainty and increase confidence.
