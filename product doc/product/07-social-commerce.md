# Purpose

Modern customers rarely begin their buying journey on a website.

Instead, they discover brands through social media.

Midevela extends the AI Commerce Operating System beyond the website, allowing businesses to deliver the same intelligent buying experience across every social platform.

Social commerce is not a separate feature.

It is another entry point into the same Business Brain.

---

# Vision

Every message should feel like talking to the same salesperson.

Whether a customer starts on:

- Instagram
- Facebook
- WhatsApp
- Messenger
- Future platforms

the AI should already understand:

- who they are
- what they asked yesterday
- what products they viewed
- what recommendations they received
- where they stopped

The conversation continues naturally.
