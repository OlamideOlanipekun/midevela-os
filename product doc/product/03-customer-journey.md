# Purpose

This document defines the complete lifecycle of a customer interacting with a business powered by Midevela.

The customer journey is the foundation for the AI Conversation Engine, Recommendation Engine, Customer Memory, Analytics, and Automation systems.

Every AI decision should move the customer forward through this journey.

---

# Journey Philosophy

Customers do not wake up wanting to browse websites.

They wake up wanting to solve problems.

The role of Midevela is to reduce the distance between a customer's problem and the right solution.

# Stage 1 — Discover

## Objective

The customer becomes aware of the business.

Possible sources include:

* Google Search
* Social Media
* Ads
* WhatsApp Link
* Email
* Direct Visit
* Referral

### AI Responsibility

None yet.

The AI prepares to receive context when the visitor arrives.

---

# Stage 2 — Arrive

The visitor lands on the website.

Immediately the system begins collecting contextual signals.

Examples:

* Landing page
* Device
* Referral source
* Country
* Language
* Campaign
* Time

The customer is not interrupted immediately.

The AI first observes.

---

# Stage 3 — Observe

Before initiating conversation, Midevela analyzes behavior.

Signals include:

* Scroll depth
* Mouse movement
* Time on page
* Products viewed
* Search activity
* Category browsing
* Exit intent

The objective is to understand before speaking.

---

# Stage 4 — Engage

The AI determines whether engagement is appropriate.

Examples:

> "Looking for the right skincare routine?"

> "Can I help you compare these laptops?"

Engagement is contextual rather than generic.

---

# Stage 5 — Understand

The AI asks questions to understand the customer's goal.

Possible questions include:

* What are you shopping for?
* What's your budget?
* Who is this for?
* Which features matter most?

This stage builds context rather than collecting unnecessary information.

---

# Stage 6 — Qualify

The AI determines:

* Buying stage
* Budget
* Product category
* Urgency
* Customer intent
* Decision confidence

This information feeds the Recommendation Engine.

---

# Stage 7 — Recommend

Recommendations include reasoning.

Example:

> "Based on your budget, intended use, and preference for lightweight devices, these two products are the strongest fit."

Recommendations should always be explainable.

---

# Stage 8 — Compare

When multiple products qualify, the AI provides side-by-side comparisons.

Comparison should focus on customer needs rather than technical specifications alone.

---

# Stage 9 — Resolve Objections

The AI addresses concerns such as:

* Price
* Compatibility
* Shipping
* Warranty
* Return policy
* Product differences

If uncertainty remains, escalation to a human representative should be available.

---

# Stage 10 — Purchase

The AI assists without creating friction.

Responsibilities include:

* Answering final questions
* Confirming selections
* Recommending complementary products
* Guiding checkout

---

# Stage 11 — Follow Up

After purchase, the AI can:

* Confirm orders
* Share usage guidance
* Request reviews
* Recommend accessories
* Provide support

---

# Stage 12 — Retain

Returning customers should receive:

* Personalized greetings
* Relevant recommendations
* Faster conversations
* Loyalty recognition

Customer Memory ensures continuity.

---

# Stage 13 — Advocate

Satisfied customers become advocates.

Possible outcomes:

* Reviews
* Referrals
* Repeat purchases
* Social sharing

This completes the customer lifecycle.

---

# Journey Success Metrics

* Time to Recommendation
* Recommendation Acceptance Rate
* Conversion Rate
* Customer Satisfaction
* Repeat Purchase Rate
* Customer Lifetime Value
