# Purpose

This document defines how Midevela transforms a traditional website into an intelligent commerce experience.

The website is no longer a collection of pages.

It becomes an active participant in every customer journey.

---

# Traditional Website

A conventional ecommerce website typically provides:

- Navigation
- Search
- Categories
- Filters
- Product pages
- Checkout

It waits for visitors to act.

---

# Midevela Website

A Midevela-powered website continuously understands visitor intent and responds proactively.

Instead of reacting only to clicks, it interprets behaviour and starts meaningful conversations.

---

# Visitor Observation

The AI observes signals such as:

- Landing page
- Scroll depth
- Mouse movement
- Time on page
- Search queries
- Product views
- Cart activity
- Exit intent

Observation occurs before engagement.

---

# Smart Engagement

Examples include:

Instead of:

> Hi, how can I help?

The AI may say:

> Looking for a laptop that can handle programming and gaming?

Or:

> I noticed you're comparing three products. Want me to explain the differences?

Every message is contextual.

# Product Page Experience

Every product page should allow the AI to answer questions like:

- Is this right for me?
- What's the difference?
- Is there a cheaper alternative?
- Is this compatible?
- Who is this designed for?

The AI should proactively surface answers before customers abandon the page.

---

# Exit Recovery

When the AI predicts abandonment, it may:

- Offer assistance
- Clarify confusion
- Suggest alternatives
- Save the conversation
- Collect contact information (if appropriate)

The goal is assistance, not interruption.

---

# Continuous Learning

Each visit improves:

- Recommendation quality
- Intent prediction
- Knowledge coverage
- Customer understanding

The website becomes smarter over time.

---

# Success Metrics

- Bounce Rate
- Engagement Rate
- Conversation Rate
- Conversion Rate
- Exit Recovery Rate
- Revenue Influenced
- Customer Satisfaction

---

# Design Principles

The AI should feel like part of the website.

Not a popup.

Not a chatbot.

Not a support widget.

It should feel like a knowledgeable digital sales associate.

---

# Summary

Midevela converts static websites into intelligent sales environments that understand visitors, guide decisions, and continuously improve through learning.
