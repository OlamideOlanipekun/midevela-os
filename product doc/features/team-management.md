# Purpose

Team Management enables multiple people to collaborate inside a Midevela workspace while maintaining security, accountability, and operational control.

Businesses should be able to grow from a solo founder to a global sales organization without changing platforms.

---

# Mission

Enable secure collaboration without sacrificing simplicity.

---

# Workspace Model

Every business owns a Workspace.

A workspace contains:

- Team members
- Products
- Customers
- Conversations
- Analytics
- Automations
- Billing
- Integrations

Everything belongs to the workspace.

---

# User Roles

## Owner

Full platform access.

Can:

- Delete workspace
- Manage billing
- Invite admins
- Configure integrations
- Export data

---

## Administrator

Can manage:

- Products
- AI
- Team
- Customers
- Automations
- Analytics

Cannot delete workspace.

---

## Sales Representative

Can:

- Join conversations
- View customers
- Close sales
- Leave notes
- View assigned analytics

---

## Customer Support

Can:

- Answer conversations
- Resolve issues
- Manage orders
- Escalate customers

Cannot modify AI configuration.

---

## Marketing

Can:

- Create campaigns
- View analytics
- Configure automations
- Build customer segments

---

## Developer

Can:

- Manage APIs
- Configure webhooks
- Manage integrations
- Access developer settings

---

## Read Only

Can only view permitted dashboards.

---

# Permissions

Permission examples:

- View conversations
- Join conversations
- Edit products
- Export customers
- Configure AI
- Manage billing
- View analytics

- Manage integrations

Permissions are granular.

# Activity Timeline
