# Purpose

Lost Revenue identifies where businesses are losing potential sales across every customer touchpoint and provides AI-driven recovery strategies.

The goal is to surface hidden revenue opportunities before they disappear.

---

# Mission

Make invisible revenue loss visible and actionable.

---

# Core Philosophy

Every abandoned purchase has a reason.

Midevela should identify that reason and recommend the next best action.

---

# Revenue Leak Categories

Customer Behavior

- Cart abandonment
- Product page exits
- High bounce rate
- Checkout abandonment
- Repeat browsing without purchase

Conversation Signals

- Unanswered questions
- Low AI confidence
- Human takeover delays
- Conversation abandonment

Business Issues

- Low inventory
- Pricing concerns
- Missing product information
- Slow response time
- Broken checkout links

---

# AI Detection

The Business Brain analyzes:

- Customer intent
- Conversation history
- Product demand
- Pricing sensitivity
- Competitor mentions
- Session behavior

---

# Recovery Strategies

Examples:

- Send personalized follow-up
- Offer alternative products
- Recommend bundles
- Trigger abandoned cart workflow
- Escalate to a sales representative
- Improve product content
