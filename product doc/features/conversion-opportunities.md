# Purpose

Conversion Opportunities continuously identifies customers, products, and situations where small interventions can significantly increase the probability of a successful purchase.

Rather than reacting after a sale is lost, Midevela detects opportunities while they still exist.

---

# Mission

Never let a high-value conversion opportunity go unnoticed.

---

# Core Philosophy

Every visitor has conversion potential.

The Business Brain should continuously estimate:

- Who is most likely to buy
- What they need next
- When intervention matters most
- Which action has the highest probability of success

---

# Opportunity Engine

Every active visitor receives an Opportunity Score.

Factors include:

- Buying Confidence
- Intent Score
- Conversation quality
- Product interest
- Historical behavior
- Purchase history
- Time on site
- Session activity

---

# Opportunity Levels

Critical

Immediate action required.

High

Likely to convert with assistance.

Medium

Continue nurturing.

Low

Monitor behavior.
