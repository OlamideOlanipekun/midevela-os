# Purpose

Instagram Integration enables businesses to convert Instagram engagement into intelligent shopping conversations.

Instead of losing customers inside DMs, Midevela transforms Instagram into an AI-powered sales channel.

---

# Mission

Turn every Instagram DM into a personalized shopping experience.

---

# Customer Journey

```text
Customer
↓
Instagram Profile
↓
Post / Reel / Story
↓
DM
↓
Midevela
↓
Business Brain
↓
Recommendation Engine
↓
Checkout Link
↓
Purchase
```
