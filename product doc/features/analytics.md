# Purpose

Analytics transforms customer interactions into actionable business intelligence.

Unlike traditional ecommerce analytics that focus on page views and sessions, Midevela measures customer understanding, buying confidence, AI performance, and revenue influence.

---

# Mission

Help businesses understand why customers buy, why they leave, and how to improve.

---

# Core Principles

Analytics should answer:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What should I do next?

The dashboard should recommend actions, not just display numbers.
