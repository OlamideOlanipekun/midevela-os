# Purpose

AI Sales Coach continuously analyzes customer conversations, sales outcomes, and business performance to provide actionable coaching for both AI and human sales teams.

Unlike traditional dashboards, AI Sales Coach doesn't just report results—it recommends improvements and explains why they matter.

---

# Mission

Help every business sell like its best salesperson.

---

# Core Philosophy

Every customer interaction is a learning opportunity.

The AI should identify:

- What worked
- What failed
- Why customers purchased
- Why customers left
- How future conversations can improve

---

# Coaching Sources

The AI learns from:

- Customer conversations
- Purchase history
- Lost sales
- Human agent performance
- Product analytics
- Customer feedback
- Recommendation acceptance
- Objection handling

## Product Insights

# AI Recommendations

Examples:

- Add comparison table for Product A.
- Customers often ask about delivery before purchasing.
- Bundle Product X with Product Y.
- Improve response time during peak hours.
- Add more social proof to Product Z.

Each recommendation includes supporting evidence.
