# Purpose

The AI Shopping Assistant is the primary customer-facing experience of Midevela.

It replaces passive product discovery with an intelligent sales conversation that understands customers, recommends products, answers questions, removes buying friction, and guides visitors to purchase.

---

# Problem Statement

Traditional ecommerce expects customers to:

- Search manually
- Browse categories
- Compare products
- Read specifications
- Understand differences
- Make buying decisions alone

Most visitors leave before reaching confidence.

---

# Mission

Give every online store its best salesperson—available 24/7, personalized for every visitor.

---

# User Stories

As a visitor, I want to:

- Describe what I need in natural language.
- Receive personalized recommendations.
- Ask follow-up questions.
- Compare products.
- Understand trade-offs.
- Get help checking out.
- Continue conversations later.

As a business owner, I want the AI to:

- Represent my brand.
- Recommend the right products.
- Increase conversions.
- Reduce support workload.
- Learn from interactions.

---

# Core Capabilities

## Intelligent Greeting

The AI determines the right time to engage.

Examples:

- First-time visitor
- Returning customer
- Exit intent detected
- Long inactivity

---

## Product Discovery

Customers can search conversationally.

Example:

> "I'm looking for running shoes under ₦80,000."

---

## Guided Qualification

Instead of immediately recommending products, the AI asks questions like:

- Budget?
- Size?
- Preferred brand?
- Intended use?

This improves recommendation quality.

---

## Product Comparison

The assistant compares products using structured data.

Comparison includes:

- Features
- Pros
- Cons
- Price
- Compatibility

---

## Objection Handling

Handles questions about:

- Price
- Warranty
- Shipping
- Returns
- Product differences

---

## Checkout Assistance

Guides customers through:

- Cart review
- Shipping information
- Payment questions
- Order completion

---

## Cross-Channel Continuity

A conversation started on the website can continue on:

- WhatsApp
- Instagram
- Facebook Messenger
- Email

Customer context is preserved.
