# Purpose

The WhatsApp integration extends the Midevela AI Shopping Assistant into WhatsApp, allowing businesses to provide intelligent sales conversations through one of the world's most widely used messaging platforms.

Customers should experience the same intelligence they receive on the website.

---

# Mission

Turn WhatsApp into an AI-powered sales channel.
