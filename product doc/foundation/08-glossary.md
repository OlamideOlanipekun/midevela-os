# Purpose

This glossary defines the core terminology used throughout the Midevela documentation to ensure consistent language across product, engineering, design, and business teams.

---

# AI Commerce Operating System

The complete Midevela platform that powers intelligent commerce experiences across websites and communication channels.

---

# Business Brain

The unified intelligence layer that combines business knowledge, customer interactions, AI reasoning, and learning.

---

# Knowledge Engine

The subsystem responsible for ingesting, organizing, and retrieving business knowledge.

---

# Behavior Engine

Tracks visitor actions, page views, clicks, scrolling, and navigation patterns.

---

# Intent Engine

Predicts why a visitor is on the website and determines their stage in the buying journey.

---

# Recommendation Engine

Selects the most relevant products or services based on customer context and business knowledge.

---

# Conversation Engine

Manages AI conversations while maintaining context across sessions and channels.

---

# Customer Memory

Persistent information about a customer, including previous conversations, purchases, and preferences.

---

# Omnichannel

A unified experience where customers can continue the same conversation across multiple communication channels.

---

# Buying Confidence

A measure of how prepared and comfortable a customer feels about making a purchase.

---

# Intent Radar™

A real-time business dashboard showing visitor intent, buying stages, and engagement opportunities.

---

# Knowledge Sync

The process of keeping business information updated across connected sources.

---

# Next Best Action

The AI's recommended action that most effectively moves a customer toward a successful outcome.

---

# AI Sales Coach

An analytics feature that reviews conversations and provides recommendations for improving business performance.

---

# Business Intelligence

Insights generated from customer interactions, behavior patterns, and commerce data.

---

# Summary

This glossary is the canonical source for terminology used across all Midevela documentation. New terms should be added here before being introduced elsewhere.
