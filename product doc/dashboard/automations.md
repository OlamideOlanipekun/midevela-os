# Purpose
The Automations dashboard allows businesses to create, monitor, and optimize workflows that execute automatically based on customer behavior, business events, or AI decisions.
Automation transforms repetitive tasks into intelligent workflows.
---
# Mission
Allow businesses to automate growth, support, and operations without writing code.
