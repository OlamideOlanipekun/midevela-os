# Purpose
The Revenue dashboard provides a real-time view of business income, AI influence, conversion performance, and growth opportunities.
Rather than only showing revenue totals, the dashboard explains how revenue was generated and where additional revenue can be unlocked.
---
# Mission
Help businesses understand every dollar earned and every dollar left on the table.
