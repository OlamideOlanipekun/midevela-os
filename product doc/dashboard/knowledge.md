# Purpose
The Knowledge module is the central workspace for managing everything the Business Brain knows about a business.
Instead of manually programming AI responses, businesses continuously enrich their knowledge base, allowing every AI engine to provide more accurate recommendations, conversations, and decisions.
---
# Mission
Create a single source of truth for every AI interaction.
