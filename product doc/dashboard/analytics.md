# Purpose
The Analytics workspace provides businesses with real-time visibility into performance across customers, products, conversations, AI, revenue, and operations.
Unlike traditional dashboards, Analytics should explain both what happened and why.
---
# Mission
Turn business data into actionable intelligence.
